# Claims Ledger (`CLAIMS.md`)

Every substantive technical claim for `Computer Evolution Lab` (`v6.0.0`) is listed below with its epistemic tier, executable verification command, evidence artifact, and explicit refutation criterion.

## Epistemic Tiers

- **`program-audited`:** Deterministically verified by automated Node.js tests, static audits, Prettier, ESLint, and CPU conformance suites against the current commit.
- **`pedagogical-model`:** Illustrative hardware/quantum/frontier simulation models or shared family gate templates rather than cycle-accurate silicon clones (explicitly distinguished per `LIMITATIONS.md`).
